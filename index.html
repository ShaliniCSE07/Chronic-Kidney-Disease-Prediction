🧠 System Architecture & Clinical Methodology

1. Ensemble Stacking Architecture

Combines Random Forest (bagging) for variance reduction with GridSearchCV-tuned XGBoost (boosting) for bias reduction. Predicted class probabilities are averaged across models for smooth decision boundaries.

2. Class Imbalance Handling

Trained on SMOTE-oversampled NHANES adult clinical data to ensure high recall for underrepresented severe stages (Stage 4 & Stage 5) while preserving overall accuracy.

3. Clinical Biomarker Integration

Evaluates 24 clinical biomarkers including Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN), Serum Albumin, Electrolytes, Blood Pressure, and Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (ACR).
